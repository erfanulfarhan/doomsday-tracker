How the two orders differ

Release order is how it was made and how the reveals were built. If this is your first pass, use it — twists like the Winter Soldier's identity or Endgame's returns only land in the order they were written.

In-universe order sorts by when things happen on the timeline, so 1943 comes before 1995 comes before 2012. It's a rewatch order. It spoils things. It's also the only way to feel how long Steve was in the ice.

Why some entries are marked essential and others aren't

Essentials are the entries that a Doomsday plot thread runs directly through: the multiverse rules, the TVA, the incursions, and the characters confirmed to show up. Everything else is in Full MCU — good films, but you can walk in without them.

The Everything route adds the Fox X-Men films and the Netflix seasons. Deadpool & Wolverine made the Fox universe explicitly canon, so those films are now background rather than trivia.
